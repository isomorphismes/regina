# Lower-dimensional face quotient

`Regina.Skeleton` continues the Agda translation with the first half of
Regina's skeleton calculation: it turns local simplex faces into global face
classes under the transitive identifications generated by facet gluings.

Source baseline: `e3a5c7c0e0581706d9e5531b750160b322489950`.
Primary source: `engine/triangulation/detail/skeleton-impl.h`, especially
`TriangulationBase<dim>::calculateFaces()`.

## Representation

A local face is a simplex index plus a Boolean vector over that simplex's
vertices. A `k`-face has exactly `k+1` true entries. This is equivalent to
Regina's local face number, but does not copy its packed numbering tables.

For every facet that does not contain one of the face's selected vertices, the
face may cross that facet. Its complete vertex subset is transported by the
facet permutation. Repeated finite saturation computes the connected component
of each local face; deleting that component and continuing partitions all local
faces into global classes.

The public observations are:

- `face_classes`
- `face_class_count`
- `face_class_degrees`
- `face_classes_of_degree`
- `skeleton_face_counts`

The algorithm is intentionally finite and total. Its fuel is the number of
local face occurrences, which bounds the length of a simple path in their
finite adjacency graph.

## Checked examples

Reduction tests recover these standard combinatorial signatures:

| Triangulation | vertices | edges | facets |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| one tetrahedron (`ball 3`) | 4 | 6 | 4 |
| two tetrahedra glued by identity (`sphere 3`) | 4 | 6 | 4 |
| Regina's 3D ball bundle | 1 | 3 | 3 |
| Regina's figure-eight knot complement | 1 | 2 | 4 |

The figure-eight example has one vertex class of degree 8 and two edge classes
of degree 6. This agrees with its two-tetrahedron ideal triangulation. The word
"ideal" still depends on link information, which this module does not derive.

## Exact boundary of this slice

This module computes the quotient classes and their degrees. It does **not**
yet reproduce Regina's embedding order, canonical local face numbers,
component orientation, link orientability, bad self-identification detection,
real boundary components, or manifold validity. Those are separate parts of
`skeleton-impl.h` and should not be inferred from these counts.
